Balloon math

The tank runs out at balloon nineteen, the arch eats twice what you bought, and the latex you inflated at noon is sulking by dinner. Balloon math counts what a tank actually fills, what an arch actually eats, and when inflating is actually safe. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

The three tools

  • Tank fill - helium liters become a balloon count and the leftover.
  • Arch count - arch meters become clusters and balloons.
  • Float window - hours until the event become inflate-now or wait.

Labeled norms

Fill volumes: 23 cm balloon about 7 L, 28 cm about 14 L, 40 cm about 42 L. Arch: a 4-balloon cluster every 25 cm. Float time: latex about 10 h, foil about 120 h - altitude and temperature shift it. Labeled party estimates, not rules - your tank gauge and your balloons win.
